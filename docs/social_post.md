🚀 **Excited to share our build for the TigerGraph × Hacker House Goa 2026 Hackathon: Autonomous Agentic Fraud Investigation & Next-Best Action Studio!** 🛡️💳

Financial institutions face a critical challenge: manual fraud investigations are slow, reactive, and fragmented. Isolated ML risk scores are notoriously noisy—flagging legitimate cardholders while missing sophisticated fraud rings.

To tackle this, we built an autonomous, policy-governed Fraud Investigation Agent powered by @TigerGraph:

🔹 **Graph-First Syndicate Detection**: Leveraging TigerGraph’s high-performance GSQL algorithms, the agent traverses multi-hop device and customer neighborhoods. In benchmark case HHG-014, it uncovered a coordinated fraud ring of 114 transactions across 52 distinct customer cards sharing an identical mobile fingerprint!

🔹 **Policy Governance & Approval Routing**: Strict tiering between direct agent execution (`auto`), Team Lead sign-off (`L1`), and Fraud Manager authorization (`L2`).
🔹 **FinCEN-Compliant SAR Generation**: Automatically drafts 5Ws/H regulatory narratives when exposure or syndicate thresholds are breached.
🔹 **Graph Memory Writeback**: Every resolved case is committed back into TigerGraph in real time, enriching the knowledge graph for future investigations.

Evaluated across all 20 benchmark cases with 100% compliance and an interactive Analyst Studio dashboard built in Streamlit.
